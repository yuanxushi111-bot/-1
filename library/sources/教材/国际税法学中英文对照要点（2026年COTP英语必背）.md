### 三、BEPS 15 项行动计划

The 15 BEPS Action Plans refer to the Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) project, which was developed by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) and the G20. BEPS is a tax avoidance strategy used by multinational corporations to exploit gaps and mismatches in tax rules to artificially shift profits to low or no-tax locations where there is little or no economic activity. The BEPS project aims to address these issues and ensure a fairer allocation of taxing rights among countries.

Here are the 15 BEPS Action Plans:

### 应对数字经济面临的税收挑戓(第 1 项)

1. address the tax challenges of the digital economy: Examining the challenges of the digital economy for international tax rules.

Digital economy: BEPS Action 1[4] aims to identify the main difficulties that the digital economy poses for the application of existing international tax rules and develop detailed options to address these difficulties.

### 消除混合错配安排的影响(第 2 项)

2. Neutralise the Effects of Hybrid Mismatch Arrangements: Tackling hybrid mismatches that exploit differences in tax rules between countries.

Hybrids: BEPS Action 2[5] aims to neutralize the effects of hybrid mismatch arrangements used to achieve double non-taxation, including long-term tax deferral, by making changes to the OECD Model Tax Convention[6] and providing recommendations on the design of domestic rules to prevent hybrids from being a source of double non-taxation.

### 强化叐控外国企业规则(第 3 项)

3. Strengthen Controlled Foreign Company (CFC) Rules: Ensuring that profits are not shifted to low-tax jurisdictions through the use of CFCs.

Controlled foreign companies (CFCs): BEPS Action 3[7]focuses on developing recommendations for the design and strengthening of CFC rules to address concerns over the possibility of creating affiliated non-resident taxpayers and routing income of a resident enterprise through the non-resident affiliate to avoid taxation.

### 通过利息扣除和其他金融支出的相关规定限制税基侵蚀(第 4 项)

4. Limit Base Erosion via Interest Deductions and other financial payments: Preventing the use of excessive interest payments to shift profits out of a jurisdiction.

Interest deductions: BEPS Action 4[8] aims to develop recommendations to prevent base erosion through the use of interest expense, for example, through the use of related-party and third-party debt to achieve excessive interest deductions or to finance the production of exempt or deferred income, and other financial payments that are economically equivalent to interest payments. The transfer pricing (TP) guideline for debt will also be expanded to limit interest deduction; for these purposes, “debt” includes financial and performance guarantees, derivatives, insurance arrangements, etc.
